# Forks and V8's worker tasks

Code: `crates/mini_racer_engine/src/process/fork.rs` (`prepare`, the fork epoch, per-process generations); `crates/mini_racer_sync/src/fork.rs` (runner phases and the gate); `crates/mini_racer_engine/src/process/workers.rs` and `crates/mini_racer_sync/src/workers.rs` (the worker pool and its queue, `Workers::pause`); `crates/mini_racer_engine/src/sys.rs` (`worker_vtable`, `WorkerTask`); `crates/mini_racer_engine/src/process.rs` (`ensure_initialized`); `crates/mini_racer_engine/src/context.rs` (`Lineage`, `adopt`, dispatches orphaned by a fork from a callback); `ext/mini_racer_rs/src/fork.rs` (the `Process._fork` and `Process.daemon` hooks). Tests: `test/ruby/fork/`, `test/stress/fork_loop.rb`, `test/stress/fork_busy.rb`, `test/stress/fork_child_memory.rb`.

`ARCHITECTURE.md` "Forks" has the protocol: the hooks, the wait of up to 500 ms for every runner to be idle or parked and of up to 100 ms more for running worker tasks, in both platform modes and best effort, the worker pool each process starts at its first worker task, and which inherited contexts a child takes over. v8-bump step 11 has the vtable slots (6, 20, 21) that send V8's worker tasks to that pool, and the debugging skill's "Fork cases" says how a child fails when a slot is wrong. Past those:

- A forked child takes over a single-threaded context only if its runner was idle at a fork that went through `Process._fork` or `Process.daemon`; every other inherited context raises `MiniRacer::RuntimeError` (`single-threaded runner: ...`). The child never frees or waits on anything inherited: it reads atomics and try-locks only. Processes are told apart by `process::fork::epoch()`, never by pid.
- V8's idle workers wait on an Abseil condition variable with their records on their own stacks, so a child that posted to them read freed memory and aborted on Ubuntu's glibc, as mini_racer's children still do; the pool's idle threads wait on a std `Condvar` instead, and the child does not post to them either.
