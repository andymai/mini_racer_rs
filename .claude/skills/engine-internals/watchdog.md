# The watchdog and `Weak` references

Code: `crates/mini_racer_engine/src/process/watchdog.rs` (`Timers::blocks`, `forget`); `crates/mini_racer_engine/src/process/fork.rs` (the generation's registry, `register`); `crates/mini_racer_engine/src/context.rs` (`context::tests`). Stress: `test/stress/pool_churn.rb`. How a nested script's timeout is stacked in the watchdog: [timeout-scope.md](../extension-internals/timeout-scope.md).

A `Weak` keeps its `Arc`'s allocation, so nothing long-lived holds one to a disposed context: the watchdog's heap entries name a context by address, and its one weak reference to each lives in `Timers::blocks` until the context's runner exits (`watchdog::forget`), and a dead one found there is replaced by the next context at its address or dropped when its entry comes due, while the fork generation's registry drops its dead ones at the next `register`. Held to a 5 s deadline, a control block made on a thread that then exits, as every `MiniRacerRs::Pool` replacement is, left glibc's arenas holding about 1 KB of free memory per context that `malloc_trim` did not hand back and that grew for as long as the churn ran (`test/stress/pool_churn.rb`, `context::tests`).
